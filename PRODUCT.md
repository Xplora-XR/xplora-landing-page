# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Businesses in general (brands, companies, organizations) that want an immersive experience built for them: XR, VR, AR or interactive 3D. They come to the site to judge whether Xplora can deliver one and to start a conversation about a project. They are not limited to architecture or real estate.

## Product Purpose
Xplora is an XR studio. It designs and builds custom immersive experiences for clients on request, priced by proposal. The landing page exists so a prospective client understands what the studio makes, trusts that it can deliver, and gets in touch (asks for a proposal or opens a contact). Success means qualified project inquiries.

## Positioning
A studio that builds bespoke immersive experiences for any kind of business, as opposed to a self-serve tool or a niche real-estate tour vendor. Open decision: the exact differentiating claim has not been stated yet. Do not invent one.

## Operating Context
- Engagement is service-based: inquiry, then proposal, then project delivery.
- Prospects evaluate the studio mainly through its work (cases, demos, video), its process and its contact channel.
- The site is in Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Existing codebase: Next.js 15 (App Router), React 19, Tailwind CSS 4, HeroUI, framer-motion, lucide-react.
- The earlier product framing (browser-based 3D tours for architects, engineers and real-estate agencies, with texture swaps, lighting simulation and measurements) is retired. It must not be used as positioning or copy.
- Open decisions: the final list of services (whether architectural visualization stays one of them), the contact channels (form, WhatsApp, email), and the primary call to action wording.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Xplora; the logotype is written in lowercase "xplora". Wordplay on "xplorar" is part of the voice.
- A Figma file named "Xplora Visual Identity" is the source for the brand identity. The official symbol and logotype SVGs are still pending; anything in use now is a placeholder.

## Evidence on Hand
None yet. No real projects, case videos, client logos, testimonials, metrics or press are available.
- The testimonials (Agostinho, Daniel, Davi), the comparison table images and the feature claims in the current code are legacy content and must not be reused.
- Future work must use clearly marked placeholders for cases, logos and testimonials, never invented ones.

## Product Principles
1. Show, don't claim: the studio's work and demos carry the persuasion; until real work exists, keep placeholders honest.
2. Speak to any business: avoid framing that narrows Xplora back to real estate or architecture.
3. Every path leads to a conversation: the page's job is a project inquiry.
4. Immersion should be felt on the page itself, without hurting performance or access.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No product-specific standard has been set. Interactive and motion-heavy elements must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, and the document language should be `pt-BR`.
